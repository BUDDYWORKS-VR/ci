02

Spelling

There is exactly one valid spelling and capitalization. It is always written on a single line and never broken.

Correct

BUDDYWORKS

  • All caps
  • One word
  • One line
  • Incorrect Buddy Works No exceptions.
  • Incorrect BuddyWorks Permitted in code.
  • Incorrect buddyworks Permitted for package names and URLs.
  • Incorrect BUDDY
    WORKS     Permitted in first-party artwork only.

Artwork

Use logos and artwork as supplied in the presskit. Don't recolor them, and keep the original aspect ratio when scaling.

05

Association

BUDDYWORKS has strict rules about who we associate with, and how and where the brand is used.

Association means official partnerships and endorsements. Use of our brand by third parties never implies endorsement.

Official endorsements are marked as such and only appear on our website(s), our social media channels, or in official announcements on our Discord.

Even where an endorsement exists, we cannot guarantee the safety or correctness of any content maintained by third parties, at any point.

We do not endorse projects or content in these categories:

Adult themes
Content marketed to adult audiences. SFW store listings are excluded.
Politics & religion
Projects spreading political or religious agendas.
Horror
Content considered shocking or gruesome.

Questions? Reach out on our Discord, linked from buddyworks.wtf.
